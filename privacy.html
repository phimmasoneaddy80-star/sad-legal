Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. What the app does

SAD ("the app") publishes videos chosen by the creator to the creator's own TikTok and YouTube accounts and shows basic account statistics.

2. Information we access

When you log in with TikTok or Google, the app receives: your account display name and profile picture; access and refresh tokens that allow the app to publish on your behalf; and, if you allow it, basic statistics of your own account and videos (such as follower counts, views, likes and comment counts). The videos you select are read from your computer only to upload them to the platforms you choose.

3. How information is stored

All of this information is stored only on the computer where the app is running. It is not sent to, or stored on, any server operated by the app's developer, and it is not sold, shared or used for advertising.

4. How information is used

Information is used only to show your connected accounts, to publish the videos you choose to the accounts you choose, and to display your own account statistics.

5. Sharing

Your videos and account data are sent only to TikTok and Google/YouTube, as needed to perform the actions you request. We do not share data with any other third party.

6. Your choices and deletion

You can disconnect an account at any time by removing it inside the app, and revoke access in your TikTok settings (Security and permissions) or your Google account (Security, third-party access). Deleting the app's data folder on your computer removes all stored tokens and settings.

7. Children

The app is not intended for children under 13.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. The date above shows the latest update.
